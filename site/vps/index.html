What the wizard does

  1. Rents your serverIt opens an EQVPS account with your email and picks a plan. The default is $10 a month with its own IP; a $5 shared-IP plan is there too.
  2. Waits for paymentIt prints a checkout link. Pay in USDC or USDT on Base, Ethereum, Solana, Tron, BNB or Polygon, or in BTC or XMR.
  3. Installs FrenIt logs into the new server, installs everything and starts Fren as a background service.
  4. Connects your chatLog in to your AI model, pick Telegram or WhatsApp, and Fren creates your wallets on the server.
  5. Backs up your keysIt offers to save a key backup to your laptop, so losing the server never means losing funds.

Have these ready

  • For Telegram: a bot token from @BotFather. The wizard finds your user ID once you message the bot.
  • For WhatsApp: a spare number for your Fren, plus your own number.
  • USDC to fund your trading wallet once setup is done.

Questions

Who holds my keys?

You do. The keys are created on your server and never leave it. The AI plans trades but never signs them, and anything over your caps needs a confirm code from you.

Why run the command on my laptop and not the server?

Because there is no server yet. The wizard rents it, connects to it for you and sets it up, so you never have to touch SSH yourself.

What happens if I stop paying for the server?

The server shuts down and Fren stops trading. Your funds stay in your wallets, and the key backup on your laptop lets you restore Fren anywhere.

Does it start with real money?

No. Every Fren starts in paper mode with $500 a trade, 20x max leverage and a confirm code for anything over $100. You turn on live trading when you're ready.
