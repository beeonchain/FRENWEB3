The Fren brand book

How Fren looks, moves and talks. Fren is a trading agent that lives in your chats: fun on the surface, strict about safety underneath. Everything here comes from the launch site, so use it as the source of truth.

Logo

The mark is Fren's head: a speedy, winged shape with one big eye. The wordmark sits to its right. Use the full logo by default and the mark alone where space is tight (avatars, favicons, stickers).

Clear space

Keep empty space around the logo at least as tall as the eye (the circle inside the head).

Minimum size

Full logo: 72px wide on screen. Mark alone: 16px wide (favicon size).

Colours

Only black or white. Put it on white, black, Fren Blue or Soft grey. Never in blue on blue.

Don't

  • Stretch, rotate or outline it
  • Add gradients, glows or drop shadows
  • Swap the wordmark for a typed "Fren"
  • Flip the head to face left

The eye is alive

Fren's eye is its personality. On screen it follows your cursor, blinks every few seconds, and says "gm" when the site loads. When the kill switch is on, the eye stops following you. Keep the eye animated wherever you can; a still Fren should still look like it is paying attention.

Behaviour

  • Pupil moves up to 8 units toward the cursor
  • Blinks every 6 seconds
  • Pupil rests looking right, toward the wordmark
  • Says "gm" on first load, then types its first message

One key word per headline sits on a Fren Blue plaque that wobbles gently. Never more than one.

Case

Sentence case everywhere, including buttons. "Fren" always has a capital F. Use real typographic quotes and apostrophes (’ “ ”).

Parts

Few parts, used the same way every time. Outlines are 1px ink, shadows are hard and offset (never blurry), corners are round.

Motion

Things move like they have a little weight and a lot of mood: a quick ease out, a small overshoot, then rest. Everything respects "reduce motion".

Signature moves

  • Blink every 6s
  • Highlight wobbles ±1° over 2.6s
  • Typing dots before Fren replies
  • Buttons press into their shadow

Voice

Fren talks like a sharp friend who trades: short, warm, a bit cheeky, never pushy. It says what it did, in one message, and it is dead serious when money or safety is on the line.

Do

  • Keep it short: "Done. You're long gold at 20x."
  • Talk like chat: contractions, "gm", plain words
  • Say what happened, then the receipt
  • Be exact about safety: caps, codes, kill switch
  • Say "your box" and "your keys"

Don't

  • Promise profits, "guaranteed" or "risk-free"
  • Sound like a bank or a press release
  • Name the tech underneath (frameworks, models, vendors)
  • Use more than one emoji, or any in safety copy
  • Call it a bot. It's your Fren.
