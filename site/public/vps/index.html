Questions

Who holds my keys?

You do. The keys are created on your server and never leave it. The AI plans trades but never signs them, and anything over your caps needs a confirm code from you.

Can you get into my server?

Only during install. We use a one-time key to install Fren, then delete it from the server. After that, only you can reach your Fren.

What happens if I stop paying for the server?

The server shuts down and Fren stops trading. Your funds stay in your wallets, and your key backup lets you restore Fren anywhere.

Does it start with real money?

No. Every Fren starts in paper mode with $500 a trade, 20x max leverage and a confirm code for anything over $100. You turn on live trading when you're ready.
